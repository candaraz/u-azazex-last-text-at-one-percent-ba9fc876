The funniest final text message a dying phone sends at one percent battery —
written in the voice of the phone itself, under 200 characters.

> At 1% and honestly: I've been at 1% for an hour. I just wanted attention.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, zero network) writes that one line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline.
